What we do

Edelnuss Global Ltd is a Kenyan agribusiness focused on turning quality macadamia into export-ready kernels. We manage the full processing chain so buyers receive product that is consistent, carefully graded, and packed to international standards.

From nut to kernel

We dry, crack, sort, and pack macadamia with controlled moisture management and precision grading. Every stage is set up to protect flavour, texture, and shelf life.
