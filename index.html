A closer look at our work

Follow macadamia from the incoming nuts through processing, hand-sorting, grading, and export-ready packing.

Step 1 — Raw Material

Macadamia in Shell

We begin our journey with premium macadamia nuts in their natural shell — carefully sourced from trusted local growers across Kenya. Every nut is hand‑selected at source, ensuring only the finest raw produce enters our facility, laying the foundation for superior quality from the very start.

Step 2 — Processing

Purpose-Built Facility

Welcome to our modern, hygienic processing plant — designed and engineered specifically for macadamia excellence. Our facility combines advanced machinery with strict food‑safety protocols, creating an environment where precision meets purity, and every operation follows international quality standards.

Step 3 — Kernel Processing

Kernel Processing Line

Shelled kernels move through our specialised refining line where delicate machinery gently separates, cleans, and polishes each kernel. This stage removes all remaining shell fragments and surface material, preserving the kernel’s natural creamy colour, whole shape, and rich authentic flavour.

Step 4 — Hand Sorting

Careful Kernel Selection

Technology meets the human eye here — our trained team inspects every single kernel by hand. We remove blemishes, inconsistent sizes, and any imperfections that machines might miss. This personal touch guarantees that only flawless, beautiful kernels move forward in our process.

Step 5 — Quality Control

Our Sorting Team

Quality is not a checkpoint — it is our standard. Our dedicated quality‑control specialists conduct rigorous, multi‑stage verification on every batch. We assess colour, texture, size, moisture content, and purity against global benchmarks. Nothing receives our approval until it meets every requirement.

Step 6 — Finished Product

Selected Kernels

After passing every assessment, what remains is perfection — whole, creamy, premium macadamia kernels. Clean, consistent, and naturally delicious. These are the kernels our clients know and trust — ready to represent Kenyan excellence on tables around the world.

Step 7 — Grading

Classified by Size & Quality

Our kernels are sorted into distinct premium grades to match every market requirement:

  • Style 0 — Extra‑large, whole premium kernels
  • Style 1SS — Uniform medium whole kernels, consistent and elegant
  • Style 4L — Large, plump whole kernels with rich appearance
  • Style 6 Pieces — Clean, crisp broken pieces, ideal for baking, confectionery, and further processing

Each grade is carefully separated, labelled, and stored to preserve its unique quality profile.

Step 8 — Packing

Sealed for Protection

Freshness locked in — immediately after grading, kernels are packed in food‑grade, moisture‑resistant packaging, hermetically sealed to retain natural oils, aroma, and crispness. Every pack carries full batch identification, ensuring complete traceability from our facility to your doorstep.

Step 9 — Dispatch

Export-Ready Cartons

The final stage — orders are securely packed into heavy‑duty, export‑standard cartons, palletised, and prepared for shipping. From our warehouse in Meru, Kenya — our macadamias begin their journey to reach clients across continents, delivered in pristine condition, ready for the world.
