About Us

A Kenyan company dedicated to premium macadamia kernels for the world market.

Company background

Edelnuss Global Ltd is a Meru-based business that dries, cracks, sorts, packs, and exports premium macadamia kernels. We exist to connect Kenyan macadamia with international buyers who need reliable quality and export-ready presentation.

Our work sits at the centre of the value chain: after harvest, we take nuts through controlled processing so kernels leave Kenya in condition for global trade. The name Edelnuss — “noble nut” — reflects the standard we hold for the product and the way we work.

Our mission

To deliver consistently high-quality Kenyan macadamia kernels through careful moisture management, advanced cracking, precision sorting, export-standard packing, and dependable logistics — so partners can source with confidence.
